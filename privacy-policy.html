Privacy Policy

Spin Master Ultra — Rishitech Solutions

Effective Date: March 30, 2026

Spin Master Ultra (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Device and Technical Information Device type, OS version, app version, language, and crash/performance data.
Usage Information Gameplay events (for example: spins, rewards, screen views, feature usage) to improve app performance and user experience.
Advertising Data Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA/GAID where applicable), ad interaction data (impressions/clicks), and approximate location derived from IP for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
Push Notification Data If enabled, push token and notification interaction data to send app updates, reminders, and reward notifications.
User-Provided Information Any information you provide directly to us (for example via support contact).

2 How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain app features.
  • Improve gameplay, rewards systems, and app stability.
  • Show relevant ads and measure ad performance.
  • Send push notifications (if permitted by you).
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3 Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Firebase — analytics, crash reporting, backend services
  • Google Mobile Ads / Ad Networks — ad delivery and measurement
  • OneSignal — push notifications

These providers may collect and process data under their own privacy policies.

4 Children’s Privacy

Spin Master Ultra is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

5 Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We may share limited data with service providers for app functionality, analytics, ads, and legal compliance.

6 Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7 Your Choices

Depending on your device/region, you may:

  • Disable push notifications in device settings.
  • Reset or limit ad tracking via device privacy settings.
  • Request deletion of data associated with your use, where applicable by law.

8 Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9 International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, where our service providers operate.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
