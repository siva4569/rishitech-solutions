1 Support Overview

If you need help with rewards, events, notifications, or app performance, contact us and we will assist you as quickly as possible.

2 Contact Support

3 Include These Details

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your device model
  • iOS version
  • App version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Screenshots (if available)

4 Frequently Asked Questions

1. I did not receive my reward. What should I do? Please restart the app and check your rewards screen again. If the issue continues, contact support with the date/time and reward details.
2. Notifications are not working. Make sure notifications are enabled in Spin Master Ultra app settings and iPhone Settings > Notifications > Spin Master Ultra.
3. The app is slow or freezes. Try closing and reopening the app. Ensure your iOS version is up to date. If the problem continues, send device details to support.
4. Events are not showing correctly. Pull to refresh or reopen the app. Event data may update based on availability and timing.
5. How do I report a bug? Send an email to info.rishitechsolutions@gmail.com with: Subject: Bug Report, steps to reproduce, screenshot/screen recording, and device plus iOS version.
